Privacy Policy

cyanote++ · Android & iOS
EnglishTiếng Việt

Effective date: 17 October 2026

cyanote++ (“the app”) is a note-taking app with tabs, a built-in calculator and a lunar calendar. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: your notes stay on your device. We do not run any server and we cannot see your notes.

1. Information you create

  • Notes, tab names and calculations are stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • Settings (language, light/dark mode, font) are stored on your device.
  • Backup files. When you choose “Export to .zip”, the app writes a file to a location you pick (for example Downloads, Files, Google Drive or iCloud Drive). Where that file goes and who can open it is under your control. When you import a file, it is read only to restore your notes.

2. Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account, no sign-in and no analytics of its own.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google’s SDK may collect and process information such as your device’s advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA where permitted), IP address, device model, operating system, app version and ad interactions. This data is processed by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

You can limit ad personalization at any time: on Android in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads); on iOS in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and Apple Advertising.

4. Permissions

The app uses internet access only to load ads. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos. Files are opened only when you pick them yourself.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your notes remain on your device until you delete them in the app or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all app data from the device; backup files you exported are not affected and can be deleted by you.

6. Children

The app is a general-audience productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

Notes are saved using the operating system’s app-private storage. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or to your backup files may be able to read your notes, so keep your device and backups protected.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version. Significant changes will be noted in the app’s store listing.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: cyan881990@gmail.com

Nhà phát triển không thu thập, lưu trữ hay bán bất kỳ thông tin cá nhân nào. Ứng dụng không có tài khoản, không đăng nhập, không có công cụ phân tích riêng.
